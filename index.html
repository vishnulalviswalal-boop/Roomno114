📖 How to get your FREE Firebase URL (1-Minute Guide)
  1. Open console.firebase.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click "Add project", enter a project name (e.g. room114) and click Continue (disable Analytics, not needed).
  3. In left sidebar, click Build → Realtime Database → click "Create Database" (select Singapore or US).
  4. Choose "Start in test mode" (allows read & write). Click Enable.
  5. Copy the URL at the top (e.g. https://room114-xxxx-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com) and paste it into the field above!
